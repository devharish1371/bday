# Your voice-note present

Drop your recording here as **voice-note.mp3**. The gift dialog will automatically show a player. Put the words you say into **transcript.txt** for the optional readable transcript.

For M4A or another supported audio format, keep its real extension and change voiceNote in ../../content.js. Until a recording is supplied, the gift dialog shows the placeholder message.

## The final line

Edit **ending.txt** for the personal sentence revealed after the recording finishes. The current sentence is sample copy—replace it with your own memory, inside joke, or promise. The player includes pause, replay, and your optional transcript. Its decorative waveform moves only while audio is actually playing and stays still when reduced motion is enabled.
